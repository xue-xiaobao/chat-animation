## gbro-collage-broll

Parts of Chat Animation's `vox` style definition—including its editorial halftone paper-collage vocabulary, semantic color system, visual-metaphor constraints, and prompt structure—were adapted and extended from:

- Project: [`pyang5166/gbro-collage-broll`](https://github.com/pyang5166/gbro-collage-broll)
- Source revision used during development: `a1a4ee2e2abf7d44e460026b706d0c72c2cf8a91`
- License: MIT

Chat Animation does not require or invoke `gbro-collage-broll` at runtime. Its style definition has been integrated into Chat Animation and expanded with content-rich first frames, 16:9 composition, distinct first/end keyframes, Agnes motion constraints, transition-aware narration timing, and per-stage QA.
